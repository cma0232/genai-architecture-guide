## Human Oversight Is an Architecture Pattern

"A human is involved" is not enough. The system must define when the human is asked, what evidence is shown, what decision is available, and what happens after approval, rejection, correction, timeout, or escalation.

## Common Intervention Patterns

| Pattern | Best use |
|---|---|
| Pre-execution approval | Irreversible, financial, external, or privileged actions |
| Editable plan | Long-running work where direction matters before execution |
| Exception queue | Low-confidence or policy-sensitive cases |
| Post-execution sampling | Low-risk, high-volume quality monitoring |
| Human takeover | Ambiguous, emotional, legal, or safety-critical interactions |
