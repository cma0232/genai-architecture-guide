# Operations, Rollout, and Ownership

*Operate GenAI systems with SLOs, incident response, progressive rollout, and clear organizational accountability.*

<ReadingTime minutes={4} />

Production is a continuing operating model. Someone must own quality, tools, data, policies, costs, incidents, and change approval after the launch team moves on.

## Define SLOs Around User Outcomes

Useful indicators include successful task completion, grounded-answer rate, unsafe-action rate, escalation rate, latency, availability, and cost per successful task. Model uptime alone does not show whether the product works.

## Roll Out Progressively

1. Internal or synthetic traffic
2. Human-supervised pilot
3. Limited user cohort
4. Canary with rollback thresholds
5. Broader rollout after behavioral and operational evidence
